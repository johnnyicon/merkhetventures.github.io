# Merkhet Ventures — Brand Brief

## Role of this document

This is a set of grounding principles, not a fixed art direction. It should give designers and generative tools enough context to create coherently while leaving room for a surprising, original interpretation.

## Core impression

Merkhet Ventures should feel thoughtful, grounded, capable, and quietly ambitious. It is a venture platform for work across communities, systems, and technology—not a generic consulting agency, an AI product company, a capital-only fund, or a finished institutional giant.

The work should invite serious conversations with people who are already making change or are positioned to enable it.

## Visual reference points

- Reflective rather than promotional
- Architectural, spacious, and materially grounded
- Editorial typography and clear reading hierarchy
- The existing teal-to-earth gradient is a useful reference for the relationship between systems, people, and the living world
- The existing architectural hero image may be used as a reference, not as a constraint
- Light, texture, and movement should support meaning rather than provide spectacle alone

## Language and narrative anchors

- Transforming knowledge into wisdom for impact
- Individuals → families → communities → systems and environment
- Harmony, responsible transition, capability, connection, shared understanding, and practical work
- The Lao Tzu quote remains an important philosophical anchor

## Avoid

- Generic AI gradients, dashboards, or “growth hacker” visual language
- Cartoonish or game-like depictions of impact
- Literal stock-photo philanthropy, fake collaboration scenes, or invented people
- Claims of scale, team size, investment activity, or portfolio maturity that do not yet exist
- An activist, government-contractor, or capital-only fund identity

## Creative freedom

Do not merely recreate the current concept page. Use these boundaries to develop a coherent world and an original visual expression that makes the Merkhet point of view more tangible.
